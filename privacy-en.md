# Dressday Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Applies to: Dressday for iPhone (also when installed on an iPad) and Android, version 1.0.0


Dressday is made by Coditive. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your clothes, your full-length photo and your wardrobe stay on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Only two things use the internet: the weather forecast and purchases through the App Store or Google Play.

Contact: kasim@mitrabilisim.com.tr

## 1. What the app works with, and where

Everything below is created by you in the app and is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. Coditive has no server and receives none of it.

- **Photos of your clothes** that you take with the camera or pick in the system photo picker, and the cut-outs made from them.
- **Labels for each item**: category, colour, pattern, season, formality, warmth, and the price, brand and note you may add yourself.
- **A numeric image description** of each item, calculated on your device by an image model built into the app. It is used to suggest labels and to notice very similar items.
- **Your Mirror photo(s)**: the full-length photo you take or pick for Mirror, the pose points and body outline calculated from it, and the images Mirror draws.
- **Your use of the app**: outfits you save, what you wore and when, likes and dislikes, the laundry basket, weekly plans and trips.
- **Settings**: style answers, temperature unit, morning reminder time, laundry rules, theme, language and the city or approximate location used for weather.

Background removal and pose detection run on your device: on iPhone with Apple's Vision framework, on Android with models that ship inside the app. Labelling uses an image model built into the app on both platforms, and outfit suggestions are calculated by the app itself. No photo is ever sent to an online service for this.

## 2. What leaves your device, and why

**Weather forecast (Open-Meteo).** To show the weather and to plan outfits for it, the app requests a forecast from Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com). The request contains only coordinates rounded to one decimal place (roughly 10 km) and the forecast settings. If you search for a city (for example when you choose your city or plan a trip), the text you type, from two letters on, and the app's language code are sent to Open-Meteo's place search (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com). Requests use HTTPS, carry no account, device identifier, cookie or user-agent, and never include photos or wardrobe data. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo can see your device's IP address; its own privacy policy applies to how it handles requests. The app keeps the latest forecast on your device for up to 3 hours before asking again, and shows the last one marked as offline when there is no connection.

You decide whether location is used at all. On iPhone the app asks only for approximate location; on Android it requests only approximate (coarse) location. If you do not allow location, you can pick a city instead.

**Purchases (App Store, Google Play).** Dressday Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles payment and your store account; Coditive receives no payment details and no name or e-mail address. The app checks on your device which Plus products your store account owns. On Android this uses Google's Play Billing Library, which is part of the app and communicates with Google Play; Google's privacy policy applies to that processing. On iPhone, Apple's privacy policy applies.

**Things you choose to share.** When you export your data, share or save a Mirror image, or copy the diagnostics text, the app hands the file or text to your device's share sheet, Photos or clipboard. Where it goes from there is your choice; Coditive does not receive it. Exports leave out your Mirror photo unless you switch it on.

Nothing else is sent. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

## 3. Permissions

Each permission is asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it, never when the app starts. The app keeps working if you say no.

- **Camera**: to photograph your clothes and to take your Mirror photo.
- **Photo library**: not requested for adding items. The system photo picker gives the app only the photos you select. On iPhone the app asks for "add only" access when you save a Mirror image to Photos; it cannot read your library.
- **Approximate location**: for the forecast, as described above.
- **Notifications**: for the optional morning reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved.
- **Calendar** (Plus, only if you switch on "Day type from calendar"): read-only access to the titles of your events in the next 7 days, matched on your device to a day type such as work or event. Event titles are not stored, not sent anywhere, and nothing is ever written to your calendar.

The home-screen widget needs no permission. It shows only item images, the temperature, the day type and one reason, never your Mirror photo. Anyone who can see your home screen can see the widget.

## 4. Device backups

Your operating system may include the app's data in your own device backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Android backup to your Google account) if you have backups switched on. Those backups are managed by Apple or Google under your account, not by Coditive. The folder with your Mirror photos is excluded from device backups (on Android also from device-to-device transfer).

## 5. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

- **One item, outfit or trip**: delete it in the app.
- **Mirror photo**: on the Mirror screen menu or in Settings → Your Mirror photos, tap "Delete my Mirror photo" (with more than one photo: "Delete all my Mirror photos", or "Delete photo 2" and so on under a single photo). The photo, its pose points and cached images are deleted at once.
- **Everything**: Settings → Data → Delete all data removes your items, outfits, Mirror photos, history and settings from the device. Your Plus purchase stays with your store account.
- **Uninstalling** the app removes its data from the device (copies in your own device backups follow your backup settings).

Because Coditive keeps no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To stop or cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account settings.

## 6. Children

Dressday is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## 7. Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal data. In Dressday you exercise them directly in the app: everything is visible and editable on your device, Settings → Data → Export my data gives you a copy, and Delete all data erases it. Coditive holds no personal data about you on any server. If you have a question or a request, write to the contact address above.

## 8. Changes

If the app starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
